# Code of Conduct

This covers the issues, pull requests and discussions of the three Macro Grid repositories (`macro-grid`, `macro-grid-client` and
`macro-grid-plugin`).

## Expected behavior

* Be respectful and courteous. Criticize code and ideas, not people.
* Stay on topic. Keep discussions about the project.
* Be honest. Don't misrepresent your work or anyone else's.
* Give and accept feedback in good faith.

## Not accepted

* Insults, personal attacks, threats or harassment
* Spam, trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior
* Obscene or sexual content
* Publishing someone's private information without their permission
* Anything illegal

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may warn, temporarily block or
permanently ban anyone who breaks them, depending on how serious and repeated the behavior is.

To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" feature, or open an issue that says only that you have a conduct report, with no
details, so that a maintainer can arrange a private channel. Reports are handled in confidence.
