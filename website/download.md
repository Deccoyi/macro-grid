# Download

Macro Grid has two parts: the **server** on your Windows PC, and an **app** on your phone. You can also use a browser instead of the app.

## Windows server (required)

Windows 10 or 11. Run the installer and follow the steps. See [Install](/guide/install).

<DownloadList />

## Android app (optional)

Android 7.0 or newer. Get the APK from the [phone app download page](https://deccoyi.github.io/macro-grid-client/download). See [The phone app](/guide/phone-app).

No app? Any browser on your network can open the deck. See [Pair a device](/guide/pairing).

## Plugins (optional)

OBS control and icon packs are in the [plugin store](https://deccoyi.github.io/macro-grid-plugin/store/). See [Plugins](/guide/plugins).
