# Download

Macro Grid has two parts: the **server** on your Windows PC, and an **app** on your phone. You can also use a browser instead of the app.

## Windows (required)

Windows 10 or 11. Get `MacroGrid-Setup-<version>.exe` from the [server Releases page](https://github.com/Deccoyi/macro-grid/releases), run it and follow the steps. See [Install](/guide/install).

## Android (optional)

Android 7.0 or newer. Get the APK from the [app Releases page](https://github.com/Deccoyi/macro-grid-client/releases). See [The phone app](/guide/phone-app).

No app? Any browser on your network can open the deck. See [Pair a device](/guide/pairing).

## Plugins (optional)

OBS control and icon packs are on the [plugins page](https://github.com/Deccoyi/macro-grid-plugin). See [Plugins](/guide/plugins).
